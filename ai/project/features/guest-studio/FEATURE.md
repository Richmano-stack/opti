# guest-studio

**Route:** `/try`
**Index:** [PAGES.md](../../product/PAGES.md)

## Job

Guest pastes a résumé and a job description, tailors, previews, and downloads. Skips the store. Default template is Minimal.

## In

- Session-only résumé + job text.
- Tailor, preview, copy, PDF.
- Default layout Minimal; toolbar may switch after they are in.

## Out

- Template store.
- Saving a master résumé or tailored result.
- Requiring an account.
