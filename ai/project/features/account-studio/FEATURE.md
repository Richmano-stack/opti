# account-studio

**Route:** `/dashboard/generator`
**Index:** [PAGES.md](../../product/PAGES.md)

## Job

After a template is chosen. Job description, tailor, preview, PDF. Needs a master résumé only when they start tailoring.

## In

- Signed-in tailoring from the saved master résumé.
- Template from the store (`?template=`), unknown value falls back to Minimal.
- Preview and PDF of the current result.

## Out

- Browsing templates as a store.
- Editing the master résumé on this route.
- History of past tailored results.
